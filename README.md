## 👋 Thanks for stopping by!

My name is Jeevan and I'm an undergraduate CS and Math student at Rutgers University. 

Currently, I'm a part time full-stack developer for Rutgers Office of Information Technology (OIT) as a part of the Open Systems Solutions (OSS) team. Outside of OSS I am also the Electronics & Software Lead at Rutgers Formula Racing where I design and assemble PCBs, write embedded systems code, and integrate it all into an FSAE car. 

When I'm not writing code or studying Algebra/Field Theory I like to spend my days backpacking, hiking, rock climbing, and cooking!

Reach me anytime at jeevandshah@gmail.com.
